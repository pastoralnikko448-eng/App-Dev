**DETAILS/DESCRIPTIONS IN THE MOVIE:**
> : Transformers One is an animated origin story set on the planet Cybertron, long before the Autobots and Decepticons became enemies. The film follows two young bots Orion Pax who later becomes Optimus Prime and D-16 who becomes Megatron when they are still friends working as low-level miners.

> : At the start, Cybertron is a society divided by class, where many robots don’t even have the ability to transform. Orion Pax is curious and idealistic, always questioning the system, while D-16 is more serious and cautious. Their journey begins when they uncover secrets about Cybertron’s leadership and the truth behind why some bots can transform and others cannot.

> : As they push deeper into this hidden history, their friendship is tested. Orion believes in change and freedom for all, while D-16 becomes more radical and driven by anger. These differences slowly pull them apart, leading to the beginning of the legendary conflict between Autobots and Decepticons. 

> : The movie combines action, humor, and emotional moments, showing how two close friends become sworn enemies and how the fate of Cybertron is shaped by their choices.

**MY RESPONSE IN THIS MOVIE**

"*I really like Transformers One because it feels like a story about best friends that turns super serious. At first, Optimus Prime and Megatron are like teammates who joke around and help each other, and that makes it fun and easy to watch.*

*But then things change, and it kinda hits hard because you see them slowly stop understanding each other. It’s like when you and your friend argue, but way bigger. I think that part makes it really interesting, not just robots fighting.*

*Also, the movie looks really cool like seeing them finally transform and become stronger is exciting. And when they discover secrets about their world, it feels like an adventure.*

*So yeah, I like it because it’s not just action it’s about friendship, choices, and how someone can change over time.*"
