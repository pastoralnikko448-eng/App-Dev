# *MOVIE NAME: TRANSFORMERS ONE*

![Picture](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/logopedia/images/e/e0/Transformers_One.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/1200?cb=20240412032715)



**DETAILS/DESCRIPTIONS IN THE MOVIE:**
-
![Picture](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcSOIuMn_C2R8RTFV60b5TUbtC_6wsTlVWgZmF0C75XPuswjcGKyvt2I9uk&s=10)


> : Transformers One is an animated origin story set on the planet Cybertron, long before the Autobots and Decepticons became enemies. The film follows two young bots Orion Pax who later becomes Optimus Prime and D-16 who becomes Megatron when they are still friends working as low-level miners.


![Picture](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/T1_FP_120-H-2024.jpg)

> : At the start, Cybertron is a society divided by class, where many robots don’t even have the ability to transform. Orion Pax is curious and idealistic, always questioning the system, while D-16 is more serious and cautious. Their journey begins when they uncover secrets about Cybertron’s leadership and the truth behind why some bots can transform and others cannot.

![Picture](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/51b3dc8ee4b051b96ceb10de/742996d5-ff76-4bb9-a924-da7e21e9b1b7/Screenshot+2024-08-15+at+8.55.51%E2%80%AFAM.jpg)


> : As they push deeper into this hidden history, their friendship is tested. Orion believes in change and freedom for all, while D-16 becomes more radical and driven by anger. These differences slowly pull them apart, leading to the beginning of the legendary conflict between Autobots and Decepticons. 

> : The movie combines action, humor, and emotional moments, showing how two close friends become sworn enemies and how the fate of Cybertron is shaped by their choices.

# **MAIN CHARACTERS:**
## *1.OPTIMUS PRIME(ORION PAX)*
> Orion Pax in Transformers One is the young version of Optimus Prime.He starts as a simple miner on Cybertron, full of dreams and curiosity. He’s brave, sometimes reckless, and believes in doing what’s right. As the story goes on, he grows and eventually becomes the great leader Optimus Prime.

![Picture](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcTgBTo1dscx4fgkYN31ANKuH5wHTbO5Rez_brNJIikLRw8m57AgyhLvD8E&s=10)
   
## *2. MEGATRON(D16)*
> Megatron in Transformers One starts as D-16, a miner and best friend of Orion Pax.
He is strong, determined, and angry at injustice. Over time, his beliefs become darker, and he turns into Megatron, the powerful leader who opposes Optimus Prime.

![Picture](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcTShV0bBNs1zs24jCI0JYospq9Rd2hR6bA-8nZ8gHVjB-PBXAvchi9r5XY&s=10)

## *3. BUMBLEBEE(B 127)*
> Bumblebee in Transformers One is a young, energetic Autobot. He’s loyal, friendly, and a bit playful, but still brave. Even early on, he supports Orion Pax and grows into a trusted ally of Optimus Prime.

![Picture](https://dims.apnews.com/dims4/default/a35bb97/2147483647/strip/true/crop/3900x1634+0+0/resize/599x251!/quality/90/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fassets.apnews.com%2Ff7%2Fa3%2Ffb191fb5841b79c654ed8fb33114%2F2e7a9289d591423cbba8884a3269375d)

## *4. ELITA ONE(ARIEL)*
> Ariel in Transformers One is a smart and kind Cybertronian. She is strong, caring, and supportive, and later becomes Elita-1, a brave Autobot leader and ally of Optimus Prime.

![Picture](https://static0.srcdn.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/elita-one-running-in-transformers-one.jpg?w=1200&h=675&fit=crop)


## *5. SENTINEL PRIME*
> Sentinel Prime in Transformers One is a powerful and respected leader of Cybertron. He appears wise and authoritative, but his actions and decisions play a big role in shaping the conflict between Orion Pax and Megatron.

![Picture](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/character-stats-and-profiles/images/6/6a/Sentinel.webp/revision/latest?cb=20250227213445)

## *6. ALPHA TRION*
> Alpha Trion in Transformers One is a wise and ancient mentor. He is knowledgeable and calm, guiding Orion Pax and helping him on his path to becoming Optimus Prime.

![Picture](https://i0.wp.com/www.thewrap.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/AlphaTrion.jpg?resize=1024%2C576&quality=89&ssl=1)

## *7. AIRACHNID*
> Airachnid in Transformers One is a dangerous and cunning Decepticon. She is ruthless, stealthy, and manipulative, often working against Orion Pax and later opposing Optimus Prime.

![Picture](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/kfIZkl31tpY/hq720.jpg?sqp=-oaymwEhCK4FEIIDSFryq4qpAxMIARUAAAAAGAElAADIQj0AgKJD&rs=AOn4CLDAaMIKd-n8WaczEYD3SUO7boT_4g)

## *8. STARSCREAM*
> Starscream in Transformers One is a cunning and ambitious Decepticon. He is smart, manipulative, and power-hungry, often serving Megatron but always seeking power for himself

![Picture](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRNJZjrAoa_cDYtO5QAAG4-s0k8kdcQXXu6Fz-hyj78DjxLtWbkEjiSB9A&s=10)

## *9. SOUNDWAVE*
> Soundwave in Transformers One is a quiet and loyal Decepticon. He is calm, observant, and deadly, serving Megatron and opposing Optimus Prime.

![Picture](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/soundwave-transformers-one.jpg?q=49&fit=contain&w=750&h=422&dpr=2)

## *10. SHOCKWAVE*
>Shockwave in Transformers One is a cold and logical Decepticon scientist. He is emotionless, highly intelligent, and focused on experiments and strategy, working under Megatron and opposing Optimus Prime.

![Picture](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/aNq1-F-szuk/maxresdefault.jpg)

# **REST OF THE CHARACTERS INCLUDES:**
## *1. HIGH GUARDS*
> High Guard's in Transformers One are a group of elite Cybertronian soldiers. They are strong, disciplined, and heavily armed, serving as enforcers of authority on Cybertron and often operating under leaders like Sentinel Prime now they following orders on Megatron.

![Picture](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GijqV7NbYAIHJGl.jpg)

## *2. CYBERTRONIAN MINERS/COGLESS MINERS*
> Cogless miners in Transformers One are Cybertronians without transformation cogs.
They are low-status workers, forced to do mining jobs on Cybertron, and include characters like Orion Pax and Megatron before they rise to power.

![Picture](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/michaelbaystransformers/images/a/a4/The_Cogless_Miners_In_Transformers_One.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20251231225434)

## *3. 13 PRIMES*
> 13 Primes in Transformers One are the original legendary Cybertronian leaders.
They were created by Primus, each with special powers, and they represent the first and most powerful beings in Cybertron’s history.

![Picture](https://mediaproxy.tvtropes.org/width/1200/https://static.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pub/images/1000014572.jpg)

# **MY RESPONSE IN THIS MOVIE** 

"*I really like Transformers One because it feels like a story about best friends that turns super serious. At first, Optimus Prime and Megatron are like teammates who joke around and help each other, and that makes it fun and easy to watch.*

*But then things change, and it kinda hits hard because you see them slowly stop understanding each other. It’s like when you and your friend argue, but way bigger. I think that part makes it really interesting, not just robots fighting.*

*Also, the movie looks really cool like seeing them finally transform and become stronger is exciting. And when they discover secrets about their world, it feels like an adventure.*

*So yeah, I like it because it’s not just action it’s about friendship, choices, and how someone can change over time.*"
